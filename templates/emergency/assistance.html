Essential Snakebite First-Aid Steps

1. Keep Calm and Still

Immobilize the bitten limb at or below heart level. Keep movement to an absolute minimum to slow venom spread.

2. Do Not Cut or Tourniquet

Never cut, wash, or attempt to suck venom. Avoid tight tourniquets or ice, which cause severe tissue damage.

3. Remove Constricting Items

Quickly remove rings, watches, bracelets, or tight footwear from the affected limb before swelling starts.

4. Transport Immediately

Reach the nearest hospital equipped with anti-venom as quickly and safely as possible. Call ahead if possible.
